Welcome to Gym Tracker!

Your entirely private, offline-first workout companion.

Everything you log here is saved directly to your device's memory. No accounts, no subscriptions, no internet required to train.

📝

Set-by-Set Logging

Type your exercise, reps, and weight, then hit + Set.

The app will automatically remember your exercises for quick autocomplete, calculate your 1-Rep Max, and group all your sets together cleanly in the table below.

📋

Templates & Copying

Hate typing the same routine? Use 🔖 Templates to save your favorite routines (like "Leg Day").

Or use 📋 Copy Day to instantly duplicate every set from a previous gym session to today's date.

⏱️

Smart Rest Timer

Use the floating bar at the bottom to track your rests. The screen will gently flash green when time is up.

Want it automated? Go to Settings (⚙️) and turn on Auto-Start Rest Timer to trigger it the second you log a set.

📲

Home Screen Sync

Added this app to your Home Screen? Safari and Home Screen apps keep separate storage.

To move your data, open Settings (⚙️) in Safari, tap Get Day Code, and simply Load that code in your Home Screen app!
