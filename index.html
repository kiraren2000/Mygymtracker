Welcome to Gym Tracker!

Your entirely private, offline-first workout companion.

Everything you log here is saved directly to your device's memory. No accounts, no subscriptions, no internet required to train.

📝

Advanced Logging

Type your exercise, reps, and weight. Use the new RPE & Notes fields to track set difficulty and add memos like "Dropset".

The app calculates your 1-Rep Max and formats everything cleanly.

🔥

Muscle Heatmap

Scroll down to see the Targeted Muscles 3D map. As you log exercises, the app scans the names and automatically highlights the muscles you are training in red!

⏱️

Smart Rest Timer

Use the floating glass bar to track your rests. The screen will gently flash green when time is up.

Go to Settings (⚙️) to turn on the Auto-Start Timer.

📲

Home Screen Sync

Safari and Home Screen apps keep separate storage. To move your data, open Settings in Safari, tap Get Day Code, and Load it in your Home Screen app!
